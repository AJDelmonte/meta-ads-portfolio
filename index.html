Through audience targeting, campaign optimization, and performance monitoring, the campaign generated 86 qualified leads while maintaining a ₱4.12 cost per lead and a 4.29% click-through rate. The results demonstrate how data-driven optimization can improve lead generation performance while keeping advertising costs efficient.
